Before & after
your treatment

A little prep goes a long way. Here's how to get the most from every visit.

How do I prepare? (3–5 days before)

Avoid retinol, tretinoin, and strong exfoliants (AHA/BHA, scrubs). Skip waxing, laser, or hair removal on the treatment area, and limit excessive sun exposure and tanning.

The day of my appointment

Arrive with clean skin and no heavy makeup if possible, and stay hydrated. Let your esthetician know about any changes in your skin, health, or medications. Men: please shave at least 24 hours prior if applicable.

Aftercare: the first 24–48 hours

Avoid heat (sauna, steam, hot showers, workouts) and don't touch or pick the skin. Pause active ingredients like retinol, acids, and benzoyl peroxide. Keep it simple: gentle cleanser, moisturizer, and SPF.

Ongoing care

Wear SPF daily and reapply as needed, avoid direct sun exposure, and keep skin hydrated and moisturized. For advanced or corrective treatments, strictly avoid actives until skin has fully healed and never pick or exfoliate peeling skin.

When should I reach out?

Contact us with any prolonged irritation or discomfort, unusual reactions, or questions about your skin or the healing process. We're here between visits too — call (669) 260-4627.
